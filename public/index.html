Our Services

Meticulous attention to detail for every part of your vehicle.

In & Out Detail

1hr 30min

Exterior: Foaming hand soap wash, tire dressing applied, door jambs and fuel compartment wiped down.

Interior: Light surface cleaning, cupholders thoroughly detailed, windows cleaned, interior air blowout, and full vacuuming.

Shine Detail

2hr 30min

Exterior: Hand wash with foaming cleanser, plastics treated with UV protection, ceramic wax coating applied to paintwork, tire gloss finish, and thorough cleaning of door jambs and fuel compartment.

Interior: Plastic and leather areas carefully cleaned and treated, leather nourished with conditioner, carpets and mats shampooed, and full interior vacuuming.

Excellence Full Detail

4hr 30min

Exterior: Foam hand wash, plastics treated with UV protection, paintwork clay bar decontamination, hand wax for shine and 6 months protection, tires dressed with shine, engine bay cleaned, and thorough wipe-down of door jambs and fuel cap area.

Interior: Fabric seats deeply shampooed and cleaned with extractor machine, plastic and leather surfaces detailed and protected, leather treated with conditioner, complete vacuuming, and deep carpet shampooing.

Premium Interior Detail

3hr 30min

Thorough vacuuming of all carpets, mats, seats, and hard-to-reach areas including pet hair. Deep shampooing of seats with advanced extraction for stain and odor removal. Carpet shampooing and extraction to lift embedded dirt and refresh fibers.

Shine Enhancer

5hrs

Bring out the shine on your vehicle’s exterior with our Shine Enhancer. This all-inclusive service combines deep cleaning, polishing, and protective treatments designed to maximize shine and safeguard your vehicle’s paint.

3 Stage Paint Correction

8hrs

Experience our 3 Stage Paint Correction. This advanced treatment is designed to eliminate more than 85% of paint defects, bringing your vehicle’s finish to a near-perfect state.

Excellence Ceramic Coating

8hrs

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer applied to your vehicle’s paint that bonds at a molecular level, creating a durable protective layer that enhances shine and protects against UV rays, dirt, and minor scratches.

Boat Cleaning

3hr 30min

Professional boat cleaning service designed to restore shine, cleanliness, and protection for your marine vessel.
